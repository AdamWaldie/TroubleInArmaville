# Trouble In Armaville

Play Trouble in Terrorist Town with your Arma group. Trouble In Armaville recreates the fan-favourite Garry's Mod game mode in Arma 3 as a fan-made homage to TTT.

You're armed, most of the group is innocent, and a few players have orders to kill everyone else. Only the Traitors know who their teammates are. Everyone else has to work it out from what they see, what people say, and what the dead leave behind.

Following another player into a building gives you someone to search for weapons with. It also gives a Traitor a chance to kill you away from witnesses. When someone accuses them later, the group has to decide whether to believe it. A convincing accusation can get an Innocent killed without a Traitor firing a shot.

## Your role

- **Innocent:** Find the Traitors and help kill them, or stay alive until time runs out. Pay attention to who was with whom when someone went missing.
- **Traitor:** Blend in and coordinate with your teammates. Spend credits on equipment to arrange kills or cover them up. Kill everyone outside your team to win.
- **Detective:** Everyone knows you're on the Innocent team. Investigate bodies, test suspects, and use your shop to help keep people alive.
- **Jester:** Get an Innocent or Detective to kill you and you win the round. You cannot deal damage. Traitors know your role, so you'll need to fool someone else.

Your briefing explains your role, and you can reopen it with **K**. Death sends you to spectate unless another player revives you.

## Equipment and evidence

Detectives can reveal a body's role and use DNA to track a suspect's distance and bearing. Samples decay, and people near a fresh scene can contaminate them. The Enhanced Scanner improves those readings and adds weapon and time-of-death details. A Portable Tester checks a living player's role at close range.

Traitors can frame an Innocent with False Flag, copy someone's loadout and DNA with Disguiser, or destroy a body before the Detective reaches it. Dead Ringer leaves a decoy corpse when you fake your death. There's also a fake health station that explodes when another player uses it.

Both shops sell radar and defibrillators alongside their other equipment. A Detective's defibrillator restores the dead player's original role. A Traitor's recruits them onto the Traitor team. Detectives also get health stations and medical supplies.

## Playing in Arma 3

Each round picks a new arena on the current terrain, with loot to search for and airdrops to contest. Arma's weapons and ballistics give Traitors room to attack from a distance, while buildings and vegetation give them somewhere to hide afterwards.

The mission supports Altis, Tanoa, Stratis, Livonia, and Malden through separate terrain packages. Weapons, clothing, and loot draw from your loaded mods, with vanilla equipment available too.

Hosts can adjust round length, role assignment, arena size, loot, weather, and time of day. Karma carries between rounds: killing teammates can cost you starting credits in later rounds. The scoreboard tracks the round, and the role HUD includes a colourblind-safe palette and several crest styles.

## Before you host

You'll need Arma 3, access to the chosen terrain, and other players. CBA_A3, ACE3, ACRE2, and TFAR are optional. No mods are required to play on a terrain you own.

Turn **Kill Messages** off in the server difficulty settings. Arma otherwise names the killer to everyone because the mission uses Civilian player slots.

Select the mission in multiplayer to host. New players can find the guide in the map screen. Press **B** for the Traitor or Detective shop.
