# Trouble In Armaville

Trouble In Armaville recreates *Trouble in Terrorist Town* in Arma 3 as a fan-made homage to the original deception game.

TTT is a round-based multiplayer game. Most players are Innocents. A hidden group of Traitors knows one another and must eliminate everyone else. A Detective is publicly identified and gets equipment to investigate the Traitors. This mission adds the Jester, who wins when a non-Traitor kills them.

## What you do in a round

You receive a private role briefing, then play the round using Arma 3's normal movement, weapons, voice chat, and maps. The mission does not announce who killed a player. Find bodies, call them in, compare accounts, and decide when you have enough information to act.

The main roles are:

- **Innocent:** identify and kill the Traitors while staying alive. You have the numbers, but not the information.
- **Traitor:** coordinate with the other Traitors, buy equipment with credits, and remove the Innocents without exposing the team.
- **Detective:** lead the investigation. You are public, so both sides can use your decisions. Your equipment includes a Portable Tester, DNA Scanner, Enhanced Scanner, radar, health stations, and defibrillators.
- **Jester:** cannot deal damage and wins when a non-Traitor kills them. The mission tells Traitors who the Jester is.

## Investigation and counter-investigation

Kills can leave DNA on bodies and nearby evidence. The Detective's scanner reports a suspect's distance and bearing. DNA decays over time, and other players can contaminate a fresh scene. The scanner can report contamination, but it does not turn a clue into a guaranteed answer.

The Detective can identify a body and reveal its role to the server. Traitors can remove a body or plant a False Flag DNA trail. They can copy another player's loadout with Disguiser, leave a Dead Ringer decoy, or avoid leaving a useful scene in the first place.

## What Arma 3 adds

- Arma 3 weapons, optics, clothing, loot, airdrops, and ballistics.
- A new arena selected from the current terrain each round.
- Release packages for Altis, Tanoa, Stratis, Livonia, and Malden.
- Equipment discovered from the mods loaded on the server, with vanilla fallbacks when those mods are missing.
- Karma that carries between rounds.
- A private briefing you can review, a dedicated in-round scoreboard, multiple role-crest styles, and a colourblind-safe palette.

Arma 3 is required. CBA_A3, ACE3, ACRE2, and TFAR are optional. The mission detects them when available but does not depend on them.

## Hosting note

Turn **Kill Messages** off in the server difficulty settings. All player slots use Arma's Civilian side, so the engine's default kill notification can reveal the killer.

Subscribe, select one of the included terrain missions, and host a multiplayer session.
